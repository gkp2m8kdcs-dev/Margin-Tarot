1. Overview

Margin Tarot ("we", "our", "the app") is a single-user iOS application designed as a quiet companion for tarot reflection and learning. Your privacy is central to the design of this app. This app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

All of your content — readings, journal entries, saved reflections, custom cards, and settings — is stored locally on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no remote server that holds your information.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Specifically, Margin Tarot does not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or contact information
  • Account credentials or authentication data
  • Precise or coarse location data
  • Device identifiers used for tracking
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or session recordings
  • Crash reports tied to your identity
  • Reading content, journal entries, or custom cards
  • Photos, images, or other media from your library

Any data you create within the app remains exclusively on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear your data from Settings.

3. Local Storage

Margin Tarot stores the following data only on your device:

  • Your saved readings and the personal reflections attached to them
  • Your journal entries
  • Custom cards you have captured or imported (image and metadata)
  • App preferences and settings

You may clear this content at any time from within the Settings screen. Deleting the app from your device will permanently remove all locally stored data.

4. System Permissions

Margin Tarot requests the following system permissions only when you initiate the relevant action. The app never accesses these resources in the background.

  • Camera — requested only when you choose to photograph a custom card using the in-app capture flow.
  • Photo Library (read) — requested only when you choose to import an image for a custom card from your Photos library.
  • Photo Library (write) — requested only when you choose to save a reading snapshot back to your Photos library.

You may decline any permission and the app will continue to function with the affected features disabled. You may also revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Margin Tarot does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, marketing, or social networking services. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the app that collect data about you.

6. Analytics & Tracking

The app does not perform any analytics, tracking, fingerprinting, or profiling. We do not display advertising. We do not cross-reference your activity with any external dataset.

7. Children's Privacy

Margin Tarot is suitable for a wide audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever make a material change to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Because this app does not hold any personal data about you, we have no way to notify you directly of changes.

9. Your Rights

Because Margin Tarot does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers, there is nothing for us to access, modify, or delete on your behalf. All data is yours and lives on your device.

If you wish to erase all app data, use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or simply delete the app from your device.
