About Margin Tarot

Margin Tarot brings the full 78-card tarot to your iPhone as a quiet companion for reflection, learning, and creative exploration. It is designed for the moments when you want a card to listen with you — a daily pause, a gentle question, a still moment before you decide.

The library holds every card of the Major and Minor Arcana, each with a hand-rendered design, upright and reversed meanings, keywords, astrological association, symbol description, and a short affirmation you can carry with you.

What You Can Do

Full 78-Card Library

Browse by suit, search by name, and tap any card to study it deeply with rich symbolism and meanings.

Five Spreads

Single card, three-card line, five-card cross, ten-card Celtic Cross, and a focused love spread.

Custom Cards

Bring your own physical deck into the app by photographing or importing images of your cards.

Daily Card

A rotating daily card offers a single point of attention to begin each day with intention.

Reading Journal

Save each reading with a personal reflection and revisit your journey over time.

Learn the Cards

A clear map of the four suits and a guide to each spread layout for beginners and seasoned readers.

How Readings Work

Each reading opens with a question you write yourself. Cards are revealed one at a time, so you can sit with each one before turning the next. When the spread is complete, you can save the reading along with your personal reflection for later — building a private journal of moments you chose to listen.

Five Available Spreads

  • Single guiding card — one point of focus
  • Three-card line — past, present, and future
  • Five-card cross — a fuller situation reading
  • Ten-card Celtic Cross — depth and texture
  • Love spread — a focused relational reading

Privacy by Design

There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, and no data that leaves your device. Readings, journal entries, and custom cards are stored locally on your iPhone and can be cleared at any time from Settings. Margin Tarot does not request, collect, or transmit any personal information.

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to capture a custom card or save a reading snapshot — never in the background.

A Tool for Reflection

Margin Tarot is a tool for reflection, not prediction. The cards do not decide for you; they sit with you while you do.
